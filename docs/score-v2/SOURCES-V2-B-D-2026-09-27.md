### B.0 Scope notes (read first)
- **2023 Ram ProMaster EV (US): does not exist.** **FACT:** Stellantis introduced the ProMaster EV as an all-new vehicle on 2024-01-16, and 2024MY was the Delivery/step van only ([Stellantis](https://media.stellantisnorthamerica.com/newsrelease.do?id=25617&mid=1548)). NHTSA's 2023 RAM model list has no PROMASTER BEV ([API](https://api.nhtsa.gov/products/vehicle/models?modelYear=2023&make=RAM&issueType=r)). `recallsByVehicle` for PROMASTER BEV 2023 returns 0. Recall 24V715 covers "2024 Ram Promaster BEV". None of the 34 ProMaster EV rows in our 2026-09-24 listing sample is MY2023. **INFERENCE:** no US MY2023 exists, so the preview row "2023 ProMaster EV" should become 2024 or be removed.
- **BrightDrop rebrand.** **FACT:** the van was the BrightDrop Zevo 600 (make BRIGHTDROP) for MY2023–2024 and became the **Chevrolet BrightDrop 600** for MY2025 ([GM spec sheet MY25, published 2024-10-18](https://californiahvip.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/AL-MY25-BrightDrop-400-and-BrightDrop-600-2025-Spec-Sheet-241024.pdf); [GM Authority 2025-08](https://gmauthority.com/blog/2025/08/some-chevy-brightdrop-units-need-an-electric-drive-transmission-module-replacement/)).
- **HUMMER EV 3X is a pickup trim.** **FACT:** GMC's 2023-04-21 release names "2023 GMC HUMMER EV 3X Pickup". A 3X trim also exists on the 2024 HUMMER EV SUV ([Automotive World / GMC release](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/the-supertruck-revolution-continues-gmc-debuts-3x-trim-for-hummer-ev-pickup-and-suv-announces-driving-range-estimates/)).
- **No EPA ratings for heavy vans and trucks.** **FACT:** fueleconomy.gov (REST menu, checked 2026-09-27) has no listing for the 2023 E-Transit, 2024 ProMaster EV, 2025 BrightDrop, 2024 Cybertruck or 2023 Hummer EV. **Maintenance ¢/mi. Class-level only; no model-specific figures exist in the sources checked.**
- **FACT:** AAA *Your Driving Costs 2026* (brochure, published 2026-09, [PDF](https://newsroom.aaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/8-YDC-Brochure_2026-1.pdf)) gives pickup-truck maintenance, repair & tires at 15k mi/yr: **Electric $1,618/yr**, Gas $1,788/yr, Hybrid $1,539/yr. The same brochure lists the category averages as ½-ton pickup 11.82¢/mi and midsize pickup 11.26¢/mi. AAA's figure includes a comprehensive extended warranty and one set of tires.
- **INFERENCE (arithmetic):** $1,618 / 15,000 mi = **10.79¢/mi** for an EV pickup (gas pickup = 11.92¢/mi). This class-level value is applied to every pickup row, labeled INFERENCE.
- **FACT:** AAA 2025 put the Electric category at **10.07¢/mi** and ½-ton pickups at 11.36¢/mi ([2025 brochure](https://newsroom.aaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/AAA-Brochure-Your-Driving-Cost-9.2025.pdf)).
- **FACT:** Argonne's 2021 TCO study, via DOE FOTW #1190 (2021-06-14, [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/articles/fotw-1190-june-14-2021-battery-electric-vehicles-have-lower-scheduled)), puts **scheduled maintenance only** at 6.1¢/mi for a light-duty BEV and 10.1¢/mi for an ICEV.
- **Vans:** UNKNOWN. We found no van-class EV figure. Candidate proxies (labeled) are in `b_maintenance_class.csv`.
- **Not used:** Consumer Reports. The prior-research PDF was not re-read today, so no CR number is quoted.

**Degradation data. Class-level only; model-specific SOH curves are UNKNOWN for all 11 preview vehicles.**
- **FACT:** Geotab (published 2026-04-28, 22,700 EVs across 21 models, [blog](https://www.geotab.com/blog/ev-battery-health/)): average capacity loss is **2.3%/yr**. Vans and other multi-purpose vehicles average **2.7%/yr**. Hot climates add **+0.4%/yr** (**INFERENCE:** this applies to Florida). Low DC fast-charging use averages 1.5%/yr (88% SOH projected at 8 yr); frequent high-power DC fast charging averages 3.0%/yr (76% at 8 yr).
- **FACT (reused):** Recurrent reports ~97% range retained at 3 yr and ~95% at 5 yr. PG&E plans for 5–10% degradation over 5 yr (URLs in `SOURCES.md`).
- **Lightning:** owner and press anecdotes (for example, 97% SOH at 93k mi) exist but are not cited as FACT because they are single vehicles.

**Known failure patterns. FACT from recall and service-program records only; see B.2.**
- **Lightning 2022–23:** high-voltage battery cell internal short (25V131, fire risk; owners told to charge to 80% until repaired); high-voltage junction-box bus-bar fasteners (24V144, then 25V272 for incorrect prior repairs); integrated park module may not lock in Park (25V863); ESC software (23V801/25V313); front upper ball joint on 2023 (24V949).
- **E-Transit 2022–25:** left rear half-shaft can partially disengage from the drive unit (25V860; ~32,160 vans; Ford estimates ~5% actually have the defect, per [Astro Ford recall summary](https://www.astroford.com/recalls/ford-e-transit-recalls.htm), secondary).
- **ProMaster BEV 2024:** motor-control software (24V715); electric drive module software (25V665, which also covers 2025).
- **Silverado EV 2024:** rear drive unit motor insulation (24V320); seat-belt bolts (23V786, 25V015).
- **Silverado/Sierra EV 2026:** high-voltage battery internal module not properly secured (26V494); steel wheels cracking (26V496); ESC warning software (25V594).
- **Cybertruck 2024:** drive inverter fault causing loss of power (24V832); accelerator pedal pad (24V276); light bar/applique detaching (24V457, 25V170, 25V735); 18" steel-wheel stud holes (26V255).
- **BrightDrop:** the 2025 Chevrolet BrightDrop 600 has **no NHTSA recalls returned** under any name variant. **FACT (secondary):** it is included in GM Customer Satisfaction Program N252502891, which replaces the rear electric drive module (differential nut may be cross-threaded or missing). This is a service program, not a recall ([GM Authority 2025-08](https://gmauthority.com/blog/2025/08/some-chevy-brightdrop-units-need-an-electric-drive-transmission-module-replacement/)). The 2023–24 Zevo 600 had recalls 23V683 and 25V156 (not MY2025).
- **HUMMER EV 2023:** high-voltage battery enclosure (22V771, which also covered the 2022 BrightDrop EV600); front seat-belt anchor bolts (23V786).
