Labels: **FACT** means the value is quoted or taken directly from the linked source. **INF** means it is derived, a proxy, or anecdotal. **UNKNOWN** means it was searched for and not found (see E).

## Summary: key numbers
- **R1T usable kWh (FACT, Rivian):** Gen1 (2022-24) Standard 106, Standard+ 121, Large 131, Max 141. Gen2 (2025) Standard 92.5, Large 108.5 (en-CA page; the US page says 108), Max 140.
- **R1T payload (FACT):** 1,764 lb for every Gen1 config. Gen2: Dual Standard 1,984, Dual Large 2,172, Max (Dual/Tri/Quad) 1,764.
- **R1T tow (FACT):** 11,000 lb with a weight-distributing hitch (5,000 lb without WDH). Gen2 Standard and Large are rated 7,700.
- **R1T EPA range (FACT):** from 255 mi (2024 Dual Standard 22") to 420 mi (2025 Dual Max 22").
- **R1T battery warranty (FACT):** 8 yr with 120k/150k/175k mi depending on config, and a 70% capacity floor (the pack is covered if it loses 30% or more). ⚠ **The consumer warranty excludes vehicles "used primarily for business or commercial purposes".** The Commercial warranty is 8 yr/100k mi.
- **R1T recalls (FACT, NHTSA API):** 2022: 12; 2023: 8; 2024: 7; 2025: 8. That is 18 unique campaigns. **Complaints:** 66 / 38 / 9 / 10.
- **R1T 3-yr retained value (FACT):** 66.0% from iSeeCars (model-level); 46.3% from Black Book (36-mo lease residual, Mar 2025). **Used asks (internal listing sample, Sep 24-25 2026):** 2022 $43.3k-$60.3k (n=10); 2023 $55.0k-$64.0k (n=5); 2024 $60.6k-$70.0k (n=2); 2025 $68.0k-$69.4k (n=3).
- **Nearest Rivian service (INF, OSRM road miles):** Vero Beach to Orlando 108 mi; Fort Pierce to Miramar 117 mi; West Palm Beach to Miramar 63 mi. **Mobile service is available anywhere in the US (FACT).**
- **Maintenance:** Rivian publishes no ¢/mi figure. Class-level numbers: Argonne scheduled maintenance is BEV 6.1¢/mi vs ICEV 10.1¢/mi. AAA 2026 gives electric pickup $1,618/yr vs gas pickup $1,788/yr at 15k mi.
- **Baseline EPA combined mpg (FACT):**
  - F-150 3.5 EcoBoost: 18-21
  - F-150 5.0: 17-19
  - Ram 5.7: 15-17 (19 with eTorque)
  - Silverado 5.3: 17-19
  - Tacoma 3.5: 20-21
  - Colorado 3.6: 19-21
  - **Transit 250/350 and ProMaster 2500 are not EPA-rated** (GVWR over 8,500 lb). Fuelly crowd data (INF): about 13-15 mpg.
- **Baseline powertrain warranty (FACT):** 5 yr/60k for every OEM. **Remaining warranty in 2026 is 0 (INF).**
- **Baseline 3-yr retained value:** iSeeCars (FACT): F-150 81.1%, Ram 74.1%, Silverado 69.6%, Tacoma 96.5%, Colorado 86.0%. ProMaster 64.8% (CarEdge, FACT). Transit 78.5% (CarResaleValue, weak source, treat as INF).
- **Baseline maintenance (FACT, class-level):** AAA 2026 half-ton pickup 11.82¢/mi, midsize pickup 11.26¢/mi.

- **FACT:** The capacity guarantee covers a pack that loses **30% or more** of its minimum usable rated capacity within the warranty period, so the floor is 70%.
- **FACT:** The consumer guide's Exclusions say the warranty does not apply if "the vehicle or product is used primarily for business or commercial purposes." **INF:** a used consumer R1T redeployed as a fleet truck could lose consumer warranty coverage. FleetFit should treat the remaining battery warranty as at risk until Rivian confirms otherwise. The guide also says coverage "may be transferable" and that subsequent purchasers should contact Rivian before buying.
- Other coverage (FACT): 12V battery 3 yr/36k mi; corrosion 8 yr.

**Degradation data. Class-level only; model-specific SOH curves are UNKNOWN for all 11 preview vehicles.**

**Known failure patterns. FACT from recall and service-program records only; see B.2.**
- **Lightning 2022–23:** high-voltage battery cell internal short (25V131, fire risk; owners told to charge to 80% until repaired); high-voltage junction-box bus-bar fasteners (24V144, then 25V272 for incorrect prior repairs); integrated park module may not lock in Park (25V863); ESC software (23V801/25V313); front upper ball joint on 2023 (24V949).
- **E-Transit 2022–25:** left rear half-shaft can partially disengage from the drive unit (25V860; ~32,160 vans; Ford estimates ~5% actually have the defect, per [Astro Ford recall summary](https://www.astroford.com/recalls/ford-e-transit-recalls.htm), secondary).
- **ProMaster BEV 2024:** motor-control software (24V715); electric drive module software (25V665, which also covers 2025).
- **Silverado EV 2024:** rear drive unit motor insulation (24V320); seat-belt bolts (23V786, 25V015).
- **Silverado/Sierra EV 2026:** high-voltage battery internal module not properly secured (26V494); steel wheels cracking (26V496); ESC warning software (25V594).
- **Cybertruck 2024:** drive inverter fault causing loss of power (24V832); accelerator pedal pad (24V276); light bar/applique detaching (24V457, 25V170, 25V735); 18" steel-wheel stud holes (26V255).
- **BrightDrop:** the 2025 Chevrolet BrightDrop 600 has **no NHTSA recalls returned** under any name variant. **FACT (secondary):** it is included in GM Customer Satisfaction Program N252502891, which replaces the rear electric drive module (differential nut may be cross-threaded or missing). This is a service program, not a recall ([GM Authority 2025-08](https://gmauthority.com/blog/2025/08/some-chevy-brightdrop-units-need-an-electric-drive-transmission-module-replacement/)). The 2023–24 Zevo 600 had recalls 23V683 and 25V156 (not MY2025).
